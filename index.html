μ / micro

Small systems.Big goals.

μSystems builds low-level infrastructure from first principles: minimal dependencies, explicit hardware interaction, and no layers that were not asked for.

Projects

μHTTP

x86-64 Assembly · Pre-Beta

A libc-free HTTP/1.1 framework in pure x86-64 Linux assembly. Raw syscalls, no runtime, no allocator. Designed as the networking layer of the μSystems stack.

Zero dependencies · Process-per-core · Focus on Security

μShield

C11 · Kernel Module · WIP

A synthetic kernel emulator and hot-patchable sandbox. Ring 0 policy enforcement with Ring 3 upcall hooks, as a lighter alternative to container runtimes and VM hypervisors.

eBPF LSM · Layered environments · Zero-copy telemetry
